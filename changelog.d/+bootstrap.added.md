Started ingot-sql, an addon of Ingots for SQL databases, for `ingots` 0.30.1 and later 0.30 releases. Its skeleton: `SqlProject`, the project of the defaults with the addon's `SqlSettings`, whose `SqlConfigs` add `SQL_LOGGING_LEVEL`, and `SqlLogging`, which adds the logger `ingot_sql` at that level; `python -m ingot_sql` runs it on its own. The development automation of the ecosystem comes with it: `make check` (lint, tests on the working tree and on the built wheel for every supported Python, coverage, changelog check), towncrier news fragments, and the `ci` and `release` workflows.
